# Version 2 artwork

The built-in image-generation tool created two original assets. These are AI watercolour interpretations, not exact photographic reproductions. They are included in the project as compressed WebP files for mobile loading; no external image service is needed when running the site.

## Couple painting — frontend/public/couple-pastel.webp

Inputs: your couple photograph as the edit target; the two stationery samples as mood and medium references only.

Prompt: Create bespoke portrait wedding stationery based on the couple photograph. Preserve their likeness, affectionate pose, clothing silhouettes and skin tones, with a soft strawberry blush dress and warm cream menswear. Full-length couple centered in the middle/lower portion, within an airy watercolor interpretation of the pond, reeds and garden. Feathery willow foliage at top corners and blush wild roses at lower corners. Mellow sage, matcha, dusty strawberry pink and milky ivory, translucent watercolor washes, fine irregular pencil lines, textured paper, low contrast and feathered edges. Upper quarter mostly empty ivory. No lettering, buildings, boat, app chrome, mockup or copied reference layout.

The couple art is absent from the cover. The revealed date page shows it at the configurable 43% opacity. The cover instead uses the botanical stationery asset. The pastel birds are native SVG paths, animated in CSS.

## Botanical paper — frontend/public/garden-stationery.webp

Prompt: Original portrait watercolor wedding stationery with pale blush wild roses, tiny cosmos flowers, soft sage/matcha willow leaves, delicate pigment blooms and fine irregular graphite linework on creamy cotton paper. Hanging foliage at top corners, asymmetric flowers at lower corners, middle 65% predominantly empty ivory for readable invitation text. Soft pastel washes and feathered edges, no people, text, frame or mockup.

The botanical paper decorates the opening letter, title-page edges, letter, venue card, RSVP background and closing. CSS veils keep it quiet behind text.

The antique rose wax seal is original native SVG in `WaxSeal.jsx`, with layered rim gradients, the shared transparent PNG stamp and a subtle grain filter. It evokes an old stamped letter rather than copying a film prop. Decorative flight, wingbeats, leaves and petals are CSS animations with reduced-motion and pause support.

The full original couple illustration now also forms the closing page. Derived responsive images, icons and social artwork are regenerated by `npm run assets`; original source artwork is retained.
